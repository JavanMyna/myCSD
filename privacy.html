Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

myCSD tracker is an early-access tool for recording your own myCSD activities and points. This page explains what data it handles and why.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name. We use them only to identify your account.

We also store the data you enter: your activities (name, points, category, date and Confirmed or Unconfirmed status), your settings, such as your target and sidang details, and the events you tick as registered.

Demo mode

If you use demo mode, your activities and settings stay in this browser's local storage. Event ticks made while signed out stay in this browser too. They are not sent to our servers; signed-in ticks are stored with your account. Exporting while signed out includes those local tick IDs as registered event IDs. Clearing site data, switching browsers or switching devices removes or hides local data, so export a copy if it matters to you.

How your data is stored and protected

Signed-in data is stored with Supabase, which provides Google authentication and our database. Row-level security is enabled so that each account can only access its own records.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, it may process technical data such as your IP address when serving pages.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It does not use cookies and is not used to track you across other sites.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. It is handled only by the services above (Google, Supabase, Cloudflare and GoatCounter), which we use to run the tracker.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any activity at any time, untick any registered event, and export all of your activities, settings, and registered event IDs as JSON from within the app.

To delete your account and all data stored with it, email javanmyna.dev@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. You can also revoke the tracker's access to your Google account from your Google account settings.

Changes and contact

This is early-access software, so this page may change as the tracker does. The date above shows the latest update. Questions or concerns can be sent to javanmyna.dev@gmail.com.
